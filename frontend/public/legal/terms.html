Orqentis Terms

These product terms describe the public preview operating model for Orqentis.

  • Customers are responsible for granting Fabric workspace access only to authorized users.
  • Orqentis enforces ODCS contracts over Delta table metadata and rule metrics; it does not replace customer governance, backup, or compliance programs.
  • AI-generated suggestions are assistive outputs and should be reviewed before activation.
  • Service levels, support targets, and incident handling are described on the support page and operations runbook.
